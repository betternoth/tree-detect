How it works

  • The page uses your camera to capture a photo.
  • It then runs an object detection model (COCO-SSD) in your browser.
  • It counts detections labeled as "tree", "potted plant", or other plant-like objects.
Works best in modern browsers (Chrome, Edge, Firefox) with camera access.
